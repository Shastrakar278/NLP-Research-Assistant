ABSTRACT
============================================================

This paper presents an NLP-based AI Research Assistant designed for literature analysis and research synthesis related to AI Based E-Learning Recommendation System. The system accepts a user-defined research topic, retrieves relevant research papers, extracts available textual information and applies NLP techniques to organize the literature. The retrieved studies are analyzed to identify methodologies, findings, limitations and possible research gaps. Based on the literature analysis, the system generates a structured research paper containing a topic-specific proposed methodology, system architecture, system workflow, expected outputs, applications, advantages, limitations and future scope. Similarity analysis is included as an additional verification step.

12. LIMITATIONS
============================================================

The limitations of the proposed approach for "AI Based E-Learning Recommendation System" are:

1. Paper availability:
   Some relevant research papers may not provide accessible PDFs.

2. Search relevance:
   Automated search may retrieve papers that are only partially
   related to the selected topic.

3. Extraction limitations:
   PDF structure and formatting can affect text extraction.

4. Abstract dependency:
   When a PDF is unavailable, analysis may depend mainly
   on the available abstract.

5. Research-gap uncertainty:
   Automatically identified research gaps require human
   academic verification.

6. Topic ambiguity:
   Very broad or unclear topics may produce less precise results.

7. Dataset limitations:
   The quality of the synthesis depends on the relevance
   and quality of the retrieved literature.

8. Generated-content verification:
   The generated research paper requires technical,
   factual and citation verification.

9. Similarity limitations:
   A similarity percentage alone cannot guarantee that
   a document is academically acceptable.
